## 1. Executive Objective
Establish a **Category-Driven Catalog Architecture** where the PDP, Search, and AI Listing Wizard consume a unified specification framework. This architecture eliminates "Presentation Logic" and schema bloat by separating Universal Attributes (Parts table) from Category-Specific Specifications (EAV-based Framework).

## 5. Success Definition
The Marketplace Catalog is certified only when:
1. **Model Parity**: Search, AI Wizard, and PDP share the same `CATALOG_CATEGORY_LIBRARY`.
2. **Deterministic Data**: No hardcoded specs, literals, or business logic exist in UI.
3. **Producer Integration**: Every PDP field is provably generated by the AI Wizard or Importer.
4. **Certified Metrics**: All Quality Gates (A, A.5, A.9, B, C, D, E) passed.
